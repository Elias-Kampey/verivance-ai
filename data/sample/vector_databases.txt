A vector database stores numerical representations called vectors or embeddings. Embeddings capture semantic meaning so that similar pieces of text are located near each other in vector space.

Vector databases support similarity search. A user's question can be converted into an embedding and compared with stored document embeddings to find the most semantically similar information.

Pinecone is a vector database designed for storing, searching, and managing embeddings. It can be used as the retrieval component of a RAG application.
